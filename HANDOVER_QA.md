# Five questions the client will probably ask at handover

**1. "What if it gets a price wrong?"**
Every PDF price is checked against the supplier's original document before it's used. On the sample price lists (two weeks from each of 3 suppliers) that was 317 out of 317 correct. A price it can't confirm goes to "to check", never into the report. And it never changes your price sheet: you approve every price.

**2. "What if my computer is off on Monday morning?"**
It runs as soon as the computer is next switched on, on battery too. You can also double-click `run.bat` any time.

**3. "A supplier changed their price list layout. Will it break?"**
It reads lists the way a person does, so small layout changes are fine. If a supplier changes completely, unmatched items show up in "to check" rather than silently going wrong. Fixing a new layout is included in monthly support.

**4. "Can I add a 4th supplier or another manager?"**
Another email address: just add it to REPORT_TO in config.env. A new supplier: make a folder for them in price_lists and put their supplier item in your price sheet. Help with that is about 2 hours, KES 2,000.

**5. "What does it cost to keep running?"**
The AI reading costs about KES 1 a week. Support is KES 2,500 a month and optional: I check the Monday runs, fix anything that breaks, and handle supplier layout changes.
