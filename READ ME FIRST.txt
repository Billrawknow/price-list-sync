PRICE LIST SYNC - READ ME FIRST
===============================

Every Monday at 7:00 am this tool reads your suppliers' price lists,
checks every price against the original document, compares them with
your price sheet, and emails you:

  - which of your items now cost more (or less),
  - which items now SELL BELOW YOUR MARKUP, with the price to charge,
  - two spreadsheets: "price_updates" and "to_check".

It never changes your price sheet by itself. You decide.


SETTING IT UP (once, about 15 minutes)
--------------------------------------

1. Put the Price List Sync folder in Documents.

2. Double-click  setup.bat
   Notepad opens your settings. Fill in:
     - REPORT_TO       : who gets the email (separate with commas)
     - GMAIL_ADDRESS   : the Gmail account that sends it
     - GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD : the 16-letter App Password for that account
   Save (Ctrl+S) and close Notepad. If anything is wrong, setup says
   which line to fix and opens Notepad again.

3. Double-click  send test email.bat
   A copy marked [TEST] goes only to the TEST_EMAIL_TO address.
   Check it arrived.

That's it. It now runs by itself every Monday at 7:00 am.


EVERY WEEK
----------

1. When a supplier sends a price list, save it in their folder inside
   "price_lists", named with the date on the list, for example:
       price_lists\kapsoit-plumbing\2026-11-30.pdf
   (PDF or Excel. Photos or scans can't be read.)

2. On Monday morning, read the email. Start with "Reprice these first".

3. Open the attached "price_updates" file and update your price sheet:
   new cost, and the suggested selling price where you agree.

4. Open "to_check" and fix what it lists, usually a product whose
   supplier code in your price sheet is wrong or that the supplier
   stopped selling. It suggests the likely fix.


WHAT THE EMAIL MEANS
--------------------

Below target markup : at today's selling price you earn less than your
                      markup for that category. "Suggested" is the price
                      that gives your full markup, rounded up to KES 10.
Costs down          : the supplier lowered the price. Keep the extra
                      margin or pass it on: your choice.
To check            : items the tool could not match. It never guesses.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

"Please fix these in config.env"
    Right-click config.env > Open with > Notepad. Fix the lines listed,
    save, then double-click run.bat.

"looks like a typo. Did you mean @gmail.com?"
    An email address in REPORT_TO is mistyped. Correct it in config.env.

"Email login failed"
    GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD must be the 16-letter App Password, not your
    normal Gmail password.

"No internet?"
    The report and spreadsheets are still saved in the "reports" folder.
    When you're back online, double-click run.bat to send the email.

"No new list for 14 days: ask (supplier) for this week's prices"
    That supplier hasn't sent a list. Ask them, save it, run run.bat.

"Something unexpected went wrong"
    Nothing was changed. Send the file logs\sync.log to support.

To run it any time:          double-click run.bat
To stop the Monday run:      double-click remove_schedule.bat
To start it again:           double-click setup.bat
